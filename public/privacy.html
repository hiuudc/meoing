Meoi Bridge v2 · Browser-only

Privacy

Meoi has no backend, MCP, OAuth flow, or database. The extension sends requests directly through the ChatGPT tab where you are signed in.

Where data is kept

  • Your workspace library is stored in this website's localStorage on your device.
  • Lessons, evaluations, and coaching returned by ChatGPT remain only in the current page's memory and disappear on reload.
  • Queued prompts and results awaiting pickup use chrome.storage.session and are removed after Meoi acknowledges them.
  • Only the mapping between a unit and its ChatGPT conversation URL is kept in chrome.storage.local.

Extension scope

The extension accepts commands only from Meoi on 127.0.0.1, sends prompts through chatgpt.com, and reads only the new assistant turn created for that operation. It does not read chat history, user messages, cookies, or internal network traffic.

Audio and YouTube

Recordings stay in the browser for playback and are not uploaded to ChatGPT. YouTube URLs are embedded for viewing; accurate source-based lessons require a transcript or notes that you provide.
