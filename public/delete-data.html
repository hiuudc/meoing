Meoi Bridge v2 · Browser-only

Delete Meoi data

Meoi has no cloud records, MCP tokens, or database entries to delete.

Delete the workspace

Open your browser's site settings for 127.0.0.1 and clear its data. This removes the workspace localStorage from that browser.

Delete extension data

Remove Meoi Bridge at chrome://extensions to delete its session queue and unit-to-conversation mappings. Temporary lessons, evaluations, and coaching already disappear when the Meoi page reloads.
