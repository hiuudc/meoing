Terms of use

Meoi is a local self-study tool. ChatGPT-generated content and evaluations can be wrong, so verify important information.

Scope

  • There is no backend, database, public service, SLA, or multi-device sync.
  • You are responsible for the material sent from the active unit to your ChatGPT account.
  • Answer keys and graders are for self-study and are not an anti-cheating system.

Availability

The local library remains available when the extension or ChatGPT is unavailable. Lesson creation, open-answer evaluation, and coaching depend on ChatGPT Web's interface and your account quota.

Stopping use

You can remove the extension and clear site data at any time. This code does not create subscriptions or cloud resources.
