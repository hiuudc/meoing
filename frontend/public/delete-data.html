Meoing - account deletion

Delete Meoing data

Request deletion from your signed-in Meoing account. Application access is locked immediately and deletion is scheduled after a 30-day cancellation window.

Before requesting deletion

Transfer ownership of collections you want another member to keep, or schedule those collections for deletion. Collection content can be restored for 30 days before it is purged.

What is removed

After 30 days, the maintenance process deletes the Auth user, profile, progress, statistics and owned R2 assets. Required audit tombstones are retained only for their stated retention period and are anonymized.

Device data and consent

Signing out first attempts to sync pending website progress. Unsent batches remain isolated to the same account and can retry only after that user signs in again. Requesting account deletion clears that account's device outbox. You can also withdraw OpenAI API consent from Learn settings without deleting your account.
