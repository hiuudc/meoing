Terms of use

Meoing is a collaborative self-study service. ChatGPT-generated content and evaluations can be wrong, so verify important information.

Scope

  • A verified account is required and application data is synchronized through Meoing’s cloud service.
  • You are responsible for the material sent from the active unit to your ChatGPT account.
  • Collection owners control membership and additive custom roles; they are responsible for granting appropriate access.
  • Answer keys and graders are for self-study and are not an anti-cheating system.

Availability

Library access depends on Meoing’s API and account availability. Lesson creation, open-answer evaluation, and coaching also depend on ChatGPT Web’s interface and your ChatGPT account quota.

Stopping use

You can remove the extension at any time and request account deletion from Meoing. Deletion has a 30-day cancellation window and follows the retention rules described in the privacy notice.
