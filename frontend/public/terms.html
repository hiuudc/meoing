Meoing - learning workspace

Terms of use

Meoing is a collaborative self-study service. AI-generated content and evaluations can be incorrect; verify important information independently.

Scope

  • A verified account is required for synchronized cloud features.
  • Collection owners control membership and custom roles and are responsible for assigning appropriate access.
  • Answer keys and graders support self-study and are not an anti-cheating system.
  • You are responsible for ensuring you have permission to submit learning material to the optional AI provider.

Availability and quotas

Library access depends on Meoing's API and account availability. API learning operations require consent and are subject to per-account and global daily quotas. Meoing may limit or disable operations when required configuration or budget is unavailable.

Stopping use

You can withdraw AI consent at any time and request account deletion from Meoing. Deletion has a 30-day cancellation window and follows the retention rules in the privacy notice.
